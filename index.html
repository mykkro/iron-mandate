PROSPECTOR'S HANDBOOK

Welcome, future workplace incident.

Destroy the rival rig before it destroys yours. Each turn, draw to five cards and gain 3 Power. Ore carries over; Power refreshes each turn.

Build machines with Ore. Each machine activates once per turn, starting on the turn after it is built. Drills produce Ore, boilers produce Power, and weapons damage the rival rig.

Your rig holds three machines and two crew. Upgrade machines for 3 Ore to improve their output. Scrap a machine for 1 Ore to free its slot. Crew bonuses stack.

Tactics cost Power and work immediately. End your turn to let the rival act. Your discard pile reshuffles when your draw pile runs out.

Win to choose a reward. In the Workshop, customize your 15-card deck, up to two copies per card. Progress saves in this browser.
